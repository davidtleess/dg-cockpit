# DG-171 — Compute TWO baselines, not one, and let contend/rebuild choose between them

**Layer:** 3 · **State:** open · **Lane:** — · **DG 3.0** · **replacement level**
**Source:** Dynasty Nerds (Liberman 2022), who computes a **starter** value and a **reserve** value in parallel:
*"it is up to each league manager to determine which method makes the most sense for them or if some ratio of the
two is best… two league managers using the same set of projections could arrive at vastly different player
valuations that would still be 'correct' with a tilt toward how much each values depth."*
Filed by Greg 2026-09-06.

---

## THE ARGUMENT THIS ENDS

Four separate published discussions argue about how deep the replacement line should sit and **none resolves it**:

- **Draft Value Analytics** names our approach a MISCONCEPTION by title — *"replacement level should be set at the
  last starter, not the last draftee. Deep benches filled with speculative picks do not move the replacement
  baseline."*
- **The Reddit author** uses *"the first man out of projected starting lineups."*
- **A commenter raises David's exact objection** — *"replacement is tough to quantify with the deep benches of
  Dynasty, you can't necessarily easily acquire the 33rd ranked RB"* — and proposes a third definition (a player
  obtainable for a mid-3rd rookie pick). **Never answered.**
- **A r/fantasyfootball thread** has the same correction made and the same follow-up left unanswered.

**David ruled 09-05: "the next who is actually available."** Measured, our bar lands at QB33-37 / RB39-45 /
WR55-71 / TE20-21 — **between** Dynasty Nerds' starter line (~QB24 for his league) and his reserve line (~QB48).
So our position is inside the defensible range, not outside it. **This is an open problem in the field, not a
mistake of ours.**

## THE RESOLUTION

Compute both. **The starter line and the deeper line are the two ends of "how much do you value depth", which is
exactly what the contend/rebuild posture already expresses.** Contending cares about the lineup; rebuilding cares
about the asset. So the posture selects the baseline rather than us choosing one for him.

## DONE WHEN

Both baselines are computed every run, both are available on a receipt, and the posture setting selects which
leads. Neither is hardcoded; both derive from his live settings.

## ⛔ TRAPS

- ⛔ **Do not let this quietly re-open his 09-05 ruling.** He ruled the deep line deliberately, after refuting two
  framings of his own. This ticket ADDS the starter line beside it; it does not replace it.
- ⛔ The measured stability of the deep bar is real and must not be lost: across 51 daily snapshots the best
  available player never changed at any position. But it IS a max over one player — see
  [[david_rulings_dynasty_asset_number_2026-09-05]] ruling 3 for the shock behaviour he authorised.
- ⚠ **His averaging idea belongs here.** He proposed averaging over a band of players rather than taking a single
  man, and it is a strict improvement: measured 09-06, three of four positions return a DIFFERENT best-available
  player depending on whether you rank by projected points or by our value score. An average does not care.
