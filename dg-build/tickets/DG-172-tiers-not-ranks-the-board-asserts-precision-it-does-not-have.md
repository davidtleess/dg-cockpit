# DG-172 — Tiers, not ranks: an ordered list asserts a precision the numbers do not carry

**Layer:** 6 (+3) · **State:** open · **Lane:** — · **DG 3.0** · **honesty on the surface**
**Source:** THREE independent lines of evidence landing in the same place, plus David's own first instinct.
Filed by Greg 2026-09-06.

---

## THE EVIDENCE, from three directions

1. **David, 2026-09-05, unprompted and before any of the reading:** *"it shouldn't be a one-through-whatever
   ranking; it should be based on a value that we give every person."*
2. **Our own reproduction, 09-05:** four adjacent pairs at the top of the asset board are separated by
   **0.02% to 0.09%** (Gibbs/Nix, Hurts/Goff, Rice/McCaffrey, Darnold/Young). The adjudicator's words: *"If the
   board is shown as an ordered list of ranks, it asserts a precision the numbers do not carry."* The same run
   found the "two players slip one place" result was this noise read as signal.
3. **Draft Value Analytics:** *"A VORP calculation can be computed to two decimal places, which creates a false
   sense of certainty in what is essentially a projection-of-a-projection."* Top projection systems miss individual
   players by **30 to 40 fantasy points a season.**

## DONE WHEN

Where two players sit inside the noise, the surface says so rather than putting one above the other. Ordering is
asserted only where the gap survives the uncertainty in the inputs.

## ⛔ TRAPS — and the hardest one is a collision between two of his own rulings

- ⛔ **He removed the band.** 2026-09-03: *"plus or minus 20, remove it, one number per player."* So a tier must
  NOT reintroduce a displayed range. It is a grouping, not a width.
- ⛔ **And his 08-31 ruling says the opposite of a blank:** *"Rank everyone, always. Confidence is a WIDTH, never
  an ABSENCE."* Those two rulings are in tension and this ticket sits in the gap. **The unresolved question is how
  to express low confidence now that the width is gone** — it is a live question for David, not a lane's to settle.
  It is currently costing him a visible thing: Jaxson Dart, his top asset, is BLANK on the asset board because his
  cell holds 10 comparable players against a publishing threshold of 12.
- ⚠ A tie-break exists if wanted: a r/fantasyfootball commenter suggests floor and ceiling to separate players with
  equal value. **Usable INTERNALLY to order within a tier; must not surface as a range.**
- ⛔ Glyph ruling applies to whatever renders this (09-04: *"glyphs and symbols, not full sentences"*), and a glyph
  invites MORE inference than words — see [[david_rulings_glyphs_2026-09-04]].
