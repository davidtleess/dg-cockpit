Where you're thin, and who you can actually get

Your confirmed question, computed: who holds a player at your position of need that they can afford to lose? It has two halves, and the first half is the one that has to be measured before the second can run — which of your nine starting slots is actually weak, against the same slot on the other eleven rosters. League Pulse carries the app's answer today and it discriminates nothing: it labels all four of your positions deficit, and prices your QB starters at exactly 0.0. Measured slot by slot it separates. The second half then shows every player at that position in the league — nothing filtered out — with what his owner would actually lose, and to whom.

What this is built on, and what it cannot see

  • Cross-position value is market units only. Comparing a QB slot to a TE slot needs one currency, and our model's DVS cannot be ranked across positions (twelve players tie at exactly 100.0 — relay 009 P4). So region 1 is a market measurement. Our lane is honest within a position, which is the only place region 2 uses it.
  • Fifteen rostered players have no model view at all (relay 009 P2: graded ACTIVE_B, returning null, stored as 0.0). They appear here with the market dot only and no model dot — including Jayden Daniels, the market's QB3, and your own Garrett Wilson.
  • The two lanes genuinely disagree about your hole, and that is worth reading, not resolving. Measured in market units against every other lineup, QB is your weakest slot and TE is your fifth-best. Our lane's positional_summary says the opposite — TE is your worst z-score (−2.72). Some of that is a real difference of opinion between the model and the market. Some of it is not: the same payload prices your QB starters at 0.0, which is the IR/taxi exclusion already filed as relay 009 P3, not a view about Jaxson Dart.
  • Built on the fresh on-disk artifact, not on what the app serves. The league endpoints still return the 2026-06-23 snapshot (relay 009 P1, undelivered), so this surface cannot ship until that lands.
  • "Costs them" is a lineup measurement, not a price. It is what removing him does to their best legal lineup today — it is not what they would ask for, and it cannot see whether they want to trade at all. Sleeper's transactions endpoint is never called by this app.
  • No verdicts. Nothing here says buy, sell or offer. Two independent quantities are drawn side by side and deliberately never fused — adds − costs is degenerate (it collapses to a team-pair constant) and adds ÷ costs is unbounded, because cost is exactly zero for any bench player.
