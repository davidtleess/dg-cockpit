One of these lanes speaks every morning. The other speaks eight times in a hundred.

Each bar is one overnight transition — how many players changed value between two consecutive daily captures, 2026-06-24 to 2026-07-30, read from the app's own capture databases.

Why this decides what belongs on the front door. The app's default screen splits into "Model output changes" and "Market movement". Measured live today, the model region renders 0 rows in 71 pixels — its copy reads "Projections held steady — no player movement on this tape" — while the market region renders 36 rows in 1,401 pixels. That is a 20:1 split of the page, and it is not a layout choice: it is what the data does.

The consequence is that the product's whole thesis is absent from its own front door on most mornings. A surface keyed to model change can only show our view on the 8% of days the model moves. A surface keyed to model position — our rank beside the market's — has something to say every single day, because a rank exists whether or not it moved.

What this is not. It is not evidence the model is broken; a dynasty valuation SHOULD be stable, and a model that churned daily would be the more worrying artifact. It is evidence that change is the wrong quantity to build the opening screen on.
