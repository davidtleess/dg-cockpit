Where the two boards agree

How to read a row. The track is the whole position, worst on the left, #1 at the far right. The blue mark is where our model ranks him; the amber mark is where the market ranks him. The line between them is the disagreement — that is the only thing on this page you cannot get anywhere else.

Where our model cannot separate players, it says so. A blue bar instead of a dot means the model gives every player it spans the identical score, so the order inside it is not ours to claim.
