Descriptive only. This page reports where two boards place a player and nothing else. It does not say which board is right, and a gap is not a recommendation. The market's youth premium may well be pricing career length correctly — the strongest argument for it is that aging is a rising chance of losing a player outright rather than a gentle decline, which a model projecting typical outcomes will always under-price.

Two things to hold while reading it. Our rank here is derived from xVAR over the 340 players on both boards; the app does not publish an overall model rank of its own, and its other number — dynasty value score — orders these same players differently (40% of them by more than 50 places). And the market board is priced for this league — 12 teams, Superflex, PPR — while the model has no league-settings input at all. Overall rank therefore compares a league-specific list against a league-agnostic one; the within-position comparison in each expanded row is the cleaner read.
