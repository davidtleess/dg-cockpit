Your league has a pulse. The app has never taken it.

Sleeper publishes every transaction your league has ever made — completed moves and trades across four seasons, free, on the same read-only API Dynasty Genius already calls eleven other ways. The app has never called it once. Below is what is sitting there.

Read this as a question, not an answer. Every number here is measured from the live Sleeper log and the running app, but the surface is deliberately rough — its job is to let you react to whether your league's own activity belongs in this product at all before anything gets designed properly. Nothing here is filtered: all twelve managers, every trade, all twelve months. The one-off 2023 startup draft is excluded throughout.
