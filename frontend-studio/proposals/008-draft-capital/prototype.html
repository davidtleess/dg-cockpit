Sixteen picks is not sixteen of the same thing.

You are last of twelve in players and first of twelve in future picks — roughly a third of your franchise has not been drafted yet. The app renders that third as a count. But the market prices your best pick at 6.4× your worst, and the biggest driver is not the round — it is which team the pick came from. Our model prices all sixteen as two numbers.

The situation, in numbers the app already has

Three facts, each read straight off a live endpoint or the market cache. Nothing here is projected, aged, or modelled.

it is three picks and a tailYour top 3 of 16 hold 41% of the portfolio's value; the bottom 8 hold 28%. All three of the top ones are your own firsts — the picks that are only this valuable while you are this bad. FantasyCalc slot ladder, cached 2026-07-24.

"a first-round pick" is not a unitThe market's own ladder falls 34% from 1.01 to 1.02, and a 1.12 (2,249) is worth barely more than a 2.01 (2,134) — a 5% gap across the round boundary everyone negotiates around. A 1.01 is 3.06× a 1.12. 48 measured slot prices, FantasyCalc.
