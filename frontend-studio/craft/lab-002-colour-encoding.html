Craft lab 002 · the encoding system · nothing here ships

A colour scheme that represents things.

Six meanings compete for hue on this product and only about five fit. So the system starts by deciding what doesn't get colour.

Every value here came out of the dataviz validator. The product's four shipped position hues fail it — and not only for colourblind readers.

01 · the findingThe shipped position hues fail, for everyone

They have never been used, so nothing has been drawn wrong yet. The moment position hue is used they would be.

QB keeps the product's own violet. Lightness varies deliberately (.52–.64) because deuteranopes separate by lightness — hue alone did not clear the target at any rotation tested.

02 · the constraintSix categorical hues do not fit, and that is measured

I searched 64 candidate position sets across every legal hue arc, varying lightness as a second channel, scoring each as one categorical set alongside model-blue and market-amber.

Result: no set clears ΔE ≥ 8 across all six. → One categorical dimension carries hue per surface. Forced, not preferred.

03 · identityPosition carries hue, and always carries its letters

The two-letter badge is not redundancy — it is the secondary encoding that makes hue legal across layers. Colour never carries identity alone.

05 · ordered ≠ categoricalPick horizon is a lightness ramp, not four hues

2026 → 2029 is ordered, and it carries a measured recency premium — a 2027 first sits ~17% above the ordinary one-year step. An ordered quantity given four categorical hues would be a category error, so it gets one channel: lightness on the ground. This spends no hue at all, which is how the system affords it.

06 · the refusalWhat gets no hue, by rule

This is the part that stops colour sliding back into decoration.

dimensionchannel insteadwhy not hue
verdict — buy / sell / holdprose plus an uncoloured lean tag This product renders decision support, not verdicts. Sleeper colours matchup quality; we deliberately do not.
manager posturerebuilding contender Verdict-adjacent. A hue would make one phase look better than another.
data absent "Not there" is not a category alongside real ones. Hatch reads as missing; a colour would read as a value.
evidence thin carrying its n Uncertainty is drawn, not footnoted — and it is not a different kind of thing, it is a weaker version of the same thing.
ownership — mine / theirsweight plus a labelled reference line Binary, and the reference line already earns its place on 013.
asset class — player / pickchip shape Form is free and unambiguous; hue is the scarce resource.

07 · the whole systemOne page, every layer, no legend

Layer 0 ground carries depth and no meaning. Layer 1 the two lanes, constitutional. Layer 2 position identity. Layer 3 horizon as lightness. Layer 4 movement, arrows only. Layer 5 everything that gets no hue at all.

the costWhat I would be arguing for, and what it breaks

It changes four shipped token values. The engineers ship four position hues today; this re-steps them. The argument is the measurement in §1, not preference — the current pair separations are 4.0 and 8.0 where the floors are 6 and 15.

It accepts one collision. RB green and WR crimson share hue families with the movement arrows. Resolved by form, place and the category's own precedent — but it is a real cost, and the alternative (positions avoiding green and red entirely) leaves too little of the wheel to separate four hues once blue and amber are reserved. I tested it.

It is restrained on purpose. Two lanes plus four positions, everything else earning distinction without hue. If the ask was "more colour", this is the defensible amount — and the honest note is that it could be argued the other way.
