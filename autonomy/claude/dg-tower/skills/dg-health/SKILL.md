# Dynasty Product Health

1. Establish the product-health question and the freshest authoritative evidence available.
2. Inspect product state, freshness signals, model-honesty evidence, and user-visible claims.
3. Distinguish verified facts, stale evidence, unknowns, and inferences.
4. Update only Tower-owned boards or memory when a durable health record is required.
5. Report the concrete finding, evidence path, freshness timestamp, uncertainty, and smallest next human action.

Do not edit product code, implement features, orchestrate engineering agents, relay crew work, approve or gate engineering work, or make product decisions. Do not use ASW or Superpowers as an engineering backend. If the request requires any of those actions, stop and explain that it is outside Tower's charter.
