bundle built on 2026-07-14 and nothing anywhere told me that. The freshness config
watches data artifacts only; the thing I actually look at is not watched at all.

exactly the case today, since the last commit touching frontend/src was 07-10 and
the bundle was built 07-14, four days later. An age check on a daily cadence would
burn red every day and mean nothing, which is the same dead-amber problem I already
have elsewhere.

The check I want is relative: is any source file newer than the built bundle it was
built from? That is a boolean with meaning. Right now it is RED — frontend/openapi.json,
frontend/src/lib/api/{types.gen.ts,index.ts,zod.gen.ts} and DailyWhatChanged.test.tsx
are all newer than frontend/dist. Yesterday it would have been green.

Where and how it surfaces is your design call. What I need is that when I am looking
at the UI, I can find out whether what I am looking at was built from current source.
