New top priority, ahead of the scoreboard. The scoreboard is still next — start this first.

Make the health signal mean something before Week 1.

Right now /api/health tells me overall_status: degraded, and every one of the eight
artifacts underneath it reports fresh. The degradation comes from capture_health and
tier_readiness, whose entire stated basis is the string "adapter_status:degraded" — no
reason, no detail. So the app tells me something is wrong, refuses to say what, and
shows me nothing but green. I cannot act on that, and after enough mornings I stop
reading it, which is worse.

And the artifact that is actually sick is one of the ones reporting fresh.
feature_refresh grades fresh on embedded_timestamp_fresh while its own
stream_provenance block records participation loading completely empty (season null,
ValueError) and pbp, player_stats and snap_counts all falling back to 2025 cache —
player_stats on a ConnectionError. Only rosters is genuinely on 2026. Nothing outside
the producer reads that block; the freshness layer grades the file's shape and never
its substance.

That is tolerable in August because 2026 has not happened. It becomes a lie the moment
games start. On Week 1 morning it is the one defect that makes my numbers wrong and
fine-looking at the same time, and the scoreboard inherits it — grading predictions
built on silently stale features is a learning loop learning from garbage.

Two outcomes I want. How you get there is yours:

1. An artifact whose inputs degraded must not grade fresh. The provenance is already
   computed and already written every run. Something outside the producer has to read it.

2. A degraded status must name what degraded and why, in words I can act on.
   "adapter_status:degraded" is not a reason.

One trap to avoid: do not solve this by making everything amber. A light that is always
degraded carries exactly as little information as one that is always green — that is the
same defect wearing the other colour. If four streams are on cache and one is empty, say
that. If everything else is genuinely fine, say that too.

To be clear this does not contradict what I told you this morning about hedging. It is
the same rule both times: tell me the truth. Do not hedge a number you actually have,
and do not call something fresh when it is not.

Do not build a governance layer around this. Then the scoreboard.
