**1 · The morning status is UI/UX: glyphs and symbols, not sentences.** He rejected a candidate sentence for
`vintage_changed_no_score_delta` (DG-150) on FORM, not on truth — he did not say it was false, and the producer
reading behind it stood. **This does NOT repeal the 08-29 prose ruling** ("I'd rather use layman's terms and call a
spade a spade", which built the whole `copy.ts` dictionary); the two are not obviously compatible and the boundary is
being resolved deliberately, not assumed away. Read narrowly until he says otherwise: a STATUS indicator on the
morning path is a symbol; an explanation a manager reads is still prose.

**⚠ The honesty trap a glyph makes worse, not better.** A sentence can hedge; a glyph cannot, and an icon invites
MORE inference than the words it replaces (a wrench reads as "we rebuilt", which is exactly the false claim DG-150
exists to remove). **Measured constraint: the morning flag's payload distinguishes NO cause** — `daily_diff.py:305`
emits `status`, `vintage_changed: true`, the window's two vintage hashes and an empty delta list, and after DG-141 (B)
the flag fires for a player's details moving, the compared population changing, a `governance_version` bump, OR a
genuine model rebuild. **So any glyph set with separate symbols for "model changed" and "player changed" is
unbuildable on today's data.** Naming a cause requires a PRODUCER change first.
